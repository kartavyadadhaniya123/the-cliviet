Our Story

Founded on a feeling, not a price tag.

As a young entrepreneur, I observed that many people admire luxury brands not only their products but for the confidence and identity they represent. However, most consumers cannot justify spending tens of thousands of rupees on a handbag.

This inspired the idea of The Cliviet — a brand that delivers elegant design, premium aesthetics, and accessible luxury for the modern woman. We believe luxury should not be defined by price alone. It should be defined by how a product makes you feel.
